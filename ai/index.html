TLC-RAPID

2026-09

Automated TLC spot detection and quantification from plate images

A deep-learning tool that locates thin-layer chromatography (TLC) spots in digital plate images with a YOLOv5 instance-segmentation model, then back-calculates sample amounts from same-plate standard calibration. It handles visible-light, 254 nm, and 366 nm fluorescence plates, lets users add missed spots by hand, and exports traceable Excel reports. Ships as a one-click Windows application.
