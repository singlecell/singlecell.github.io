Research Interests

I integrate in situ mass spectrometry, miniature mass spectrometry and artificial intelligence to analyse complex traditional Chinese medicine systems — along three directions.

AI-Driven Analysis

Machine Learning · Deep Learning

Machine learning on in situ and miniature MS data for intelligent interpretation and discrimination; deep learning for image-based quantitation.

Learn more →

In Situ Mass Spectrometry

TLC–MS · MS Imaging · Miniature MS

TLC–ambient MS, bioautography–MS screening and MS imaging identify constituents without sample preparation; miniature MS takes the analysis on site.

Learn more →

TCM Analysis

Quality · Safety · Standards

Quality evaluation and safety screening of ginseng, notoginseng, agarwood, citrus peel, Senecio and their preparations; ISO 19842:2025.
